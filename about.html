The OrenkaFine House

Our philosophy

The world is my catwalk

OrenkaFine was founded on a simple idea: fine Jewellery should be worn, not saved for occasions. Our founder began sketching pieces at café tables across Europe, gathering color and form from wherever life took her.

Today the house designs two distinct lines from the same studio — OrenkaFine Jewellery, our fine gold collection, and OrenkaFine Moda, our fashion-forward everyday pieces — united by the same hand-finished craftsmanship.

Craftsmanship

Made to be layered, mixed, and lived in

Every setting is finished by hand in small batches. We work in gold, diamond and considered gemstones — amethyst, emerald, London blue topaz — chosen for how they catch the light against skin.

We design in families, not single pieces, so a ring, a stack of bangles and a pair of earrings from the same collection always feel like they belong together.
