Orenkafine Grievance Redressal Policy

(Under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the IT Intermediary Guidelines Rules)

Effective Date: 4 September 2026  |  Last Updated: 4 September 2026

Note on legal basis (as of September 2026): This Policy is issued to comply with Rule 3(2) and related provisions of the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021 ("IT Rules"), framed under Section 79 of the Information Technology Act, 2000, as amended by the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Amendment Rules, 2026, which came into force on 20 February 2026 and shortened several grievance and takedown timelines. This Policy addresses complaints relating to information/content hosted, displayed or transmitted on the Platform (including Customer reviews, photographs and other user‑generated content) and violations of these due‑diligence obligations. It operates alongside, and does not replace, (a) Orenkafine's consumer grievance mechanism under the Consumer Protection (E‑Commerce) Rules, 2020 (see the Terms & Conditions above), and (b) Orenkafine's data‑related grievance mechanism under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (see the Privacy Policy).

1. Introduction and Applicability

Orenkafine ("Orenkafine", "we", "us", "our") operates the Orenkafine website, mobile interface and online store (collectively, the "Platform"), which qualifies as an "intermediary" within the meaning of Section 2(1)(w) of the Information Technology Act, 2000, to the extent it hosts, stores or transmits information provided by Customers, including product reviews, ratings, photographs, comments and other user‑generated content.

In accordance with Rule 3(2) of the IT Rules, Orenkafine has established this Grievance Redressal Policy and appointed a Grievance Officer to receive and resolve complaints from users and victims regarding: (a) any content or information hosted on the Platform that is alleged to violate the IT Rules or applicable law; (b) any violation of Orenkafine's Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy or other published policies; and (c) any other matter falling within the scope of the IT Rules.

This Policy applies in addition to, and does not limit, the consumer grievance mechanism under Clause 44 of Orenkafine's Terms & Conditions (for Order, payment, delivery, return and refund complaints) and the data protection grievance mechanism under Orenkafine's Privacy Policy (for complaints regarding processing of personal data).

2. Definitions

"Complainant" or "User" means any person, including a victim, who submits a grievance under this Policy.

"Grievance" means a complaint relating to content or information hosted on the Platform, a violation of Orenkafine's published policies, or any other matter within the scope of the IT Rules.

"Victim" means a person who has suffered harm as a result of content published on the Platform, including content depicting such person, whether or not such person is a registered user of the Platform.

"IT Rules" means the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, as amended from time to time, including by the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Amendment Rules, 2026.

"Synthetically Generated Information" or "SGI" means audio, visual or audio‑visual information that is artificially or algorithmically created, generated, modified or altered using a computer resource in such a manner that it appears reasonably authentic or true, as defined under the IT Rules.

4. What You Can Complain About

You may file a Grievance under this Policy in relation to information or content hosted, displayed, uploaded, modified, published, transmitted, stored or shared on the Platform (including in Customer reviews, photographs, ratings, comments or other user‑generated content) that:

  • Belongs to another person and to which you do not have any right;
  • Is obscene, pornographic, paedophilic, invasive of another's privacy (including bodily privacy), insulting or harassing on the basis of gender, or is otherwise sexually explicit or indecent;
  • Is harmful to a child;
  • Infringes any patent, trademark, copyright or other proprietary right;
  • Violates any law for the time being in force;
  • Is defamatory, or deceives or misleads the addressee about the origin of the message, or communicates any misinformation or information which is patently false or untrue;
  • Impersonates another person;
  • Threatens the unity, integrity, defence, security or sovereignty of India, friendly relations with foreign States, or public order, or incites the commission of a cognisable offence;
  • Contains software viruses or any other malicious code;
  • Is a Synthetically Generated Information (SGI) item that is not appropriately labelled/disclosed as required under the IT Rules, or that falsely depicts a person or a real‑world event;
  • Otherwise violates Orenkafine's Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy or other published policies.

7. Action on Valid Grievances

Upon receiving a Grievance, the Grievance Officer will review the complaint, may seek additional information from the Complainant, and, where a Grievance is found to be valid, will take appropriate action, which may include:

  • Removing or disabling access to the content complained of;
  • Warning, suspending or terminating the account of the User responsible for the content, where appropriate;
  • Preserving relevant records and information as required for compliance or law enforcement purposes;
  • Reporting the matter to the appropriate government agency, where mandatorily required by law (including offences under the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012 and other applicable criminal law); and/or
  • Any other action permitted or required under applicable law.

Orenkafine will make reasonable efforts, including through appropriate human oversight and, where applicable, automated tools, to identify and act on content falling within Clause 4, consistent with the due diligence standard expected under the IT Rules.

8. Synthetically Generated Information (SGI)

Where the Platform permits Users to upload or generate visual or audio content (for example, as part of a review or a customisation request) that qualifies as Synthetically Generated Information, Orenkafine will, to the extent applicable:

  • Inform Users that creating or publishing unlawful SGI (including false depictions of a real person or event, non‑consensual intimate imagery, or content contravening applicable law) may attract penalties, suspension of access, content removal, and reporting to authorities;
  • Require such content to carry a label or disclosure that is prominent, easily noticeable and adequately perceivable as SGI, where the content is visual, and a prominently prefixed audio disclosure where the content is audio;
  • Deploy reasonable and appropriate technical measures aimed at preventing the generation or dissemination, through the Platform, of unlawful SGI of the nature described in Clause 4.

9. Periodic User Advisory

In accordance with the IT Rules, Orenkafine will, at least once every three (3) months, inform Users of: (a) Orenkafine's right to remove or disable access to non‑compliant content and to suspend or terminate access of Users who violate the Terms & Conditions or applicable law; (b) the User's exposure to civil and criminal liability for unlawful content under applicable law; and (c) Orenkafine's obligation to report certain offences, including under the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012, to the appropriate authorities.

10. Escalation — Grievance Appellate Committee

If a Complainant is not satisfied with the decision of the Grievance Officer, or if a Grievance is not resolved within the applicable timeline, the Complainant may prefer an appeal before the Grievance Appellate Committee(s) constituted by the Central Government under Rule 3A of the IT Rules, within the timeframe and in the manner prescribed thereunder. The Grievance Appellate Committee endeavours to dispose of appeals within thirty (30) days of receipt.

This right of appeal is in addition to, and does not affect, any other right or remedy available to the Complainant under applicable law, including approaching a competent court, the National Consumer Helpline (1915), the e‑Daakhil portal (edaakhil.nic.in), or, in relation to personal data, the Data Protection Board of India once operative.
