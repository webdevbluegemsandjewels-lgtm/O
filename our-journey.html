Our Story

The Vision of Shweta Goyal & Gunaayaa Goyal • Backed by 30 Years of Jewellery Expertise

OrenkaFine

OrenkaFine was founded by Shweta Goyal and Gunaayaa Goyal, building on nearly three decades of jewellery manufacturing expertise from our parent company, established in 1996.

Their vision was to make fine jewellery more affordable, accessible, and meaningful for the younger generation. We believe jewellery should be more than something beautiful to wear—it should also be something of lasting value.

By offering genuine gold and diamond jewellery at honest prices, OrenkaFine encourages a smarter way to spend. Unlike many everyday purchases that lose value over time, fine jewellery carries intrinsic value and can remain meaningful for years to come.

Our collections are designed for modern lifestyles, with a focus on lightweight, elegant, certified jewellery for everyday wear—making it easier for a new generation to own fine jewellery without unnecessary markups.

Rooted in Mumbai

The Mumbai Foundation

Mumbai has always been the home of OrenkaFine.

Located in one of the world's most respected jewellery and diamond trading hubs, we benefit from generations of craftsmanship, skilled artisans, and access to the finest materials.

Our manufacturing and operations are built on precision, quality, and attention to detail. Every stage—from design and sourcing to production, polishing, and final inspection—is carefully managed to ensure every piece meets our highest standards.

This strong foundation allows us to deliver jewellery that customers can wear with complete confidence.

The OrenkaFine promise

Why Choose Us

Choosing fine jewellery is about more than beauty—it is about trust, quality, craftsmanship, and lasting value.

Backed by nearly 30 years of manufacturing expertise, OrenkaFine follows a direct-to-customer model that allows us to focus on what truly matters: delivering superior-quality jewellery at honest prices.

As direct manufacturers, we reduce unnecessary costs associated with middlemen, heavy inventory, expensive retail operations, influencer marketing, and large advertising spends. Instead of passing these costs on to our customers, we invest in better materials, skilled craftsmanship, thoughtful design, careful finishing, certification, and quality control.

Every OrenkaFine piece is thoughtfully designed, meticulously crafted, and carefully inspected before it reaches you.

Our promise is simple: beautiful fine jewellery, superior quality, transparent pricing, and lasting value—without the unnecessary costs.

The people behind the name

The Craft Behind the Name

At OrenkaFine, the craft begins with understanding the piece: its purpose, design language, material, stones, karat, finish, and the customer it is meant for.

The company works in 18K, 14K, and 9K gold, with designs made in yellow gold, white gold, and rose gold finishes. Each piece is handled with attention to proportion, setting security, wearability, polish, and commercial suitability. Our process is shaped by disciplined development: understanding the requirement, analysing practicality, refining the design, preparing materials, executing with skill, and checking the finished piece before delivery.
