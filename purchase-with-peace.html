BIS Hallmarked Gold

Purity guaranteed

All OrenkaFine jewellery meets the standards set by the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS). Every piece is hallmarked for gold purity, with its HUID printed right on your invoice.

The Hallmark Unique Identification (HUID) is a six-digit alphanumeric code, unique to every single piece of jewellery — assigned and hand-stamped at the Assaying & Hallmarking Centre at the time of hallmarking. You can look it up any time on the official BIS portal or the BIS Care app.

Our diamond jewellery carries its SGL certification alongside the gold hallmark, so both the metal and the stone are independently verified.
