Orenkafine Cookie Policy

Effective Date: 4 September 2026  |  Last Updated: 4 September 2026

Note on legal basis (as of September 2026): India does not currently have a standalone law that uses the word "cookie". Cookies that collect personal data, such as a device identifier, browsing activity or an IP address linked to an individual, fall within the definition of personal data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, together with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its Sensitive Personal Data Rules of 2011, to the extent applicable. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, consent for cookies that are not strictly necessary must be free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous, given through a clear affirmative action, and it must be as easy to refuse or withdraw as it is to give. This document reflects that standard.

1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how Orenkafine ("Orenkafine", "we", "us", "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on the Orenkafine website, mobile interface and online store (together, the "Platform"), why we use them, and the choices available to you.

This Policy should be read together with the Orenkafine Privacy Policy, which explains in general how we collect, use and protect personal data. Where a term used here is not defined, it has the meaning given to it in the Privacy Policy.

2. What Are Cookies

A cookie is a small text file that a website places on your device, such as a computer, tablet or phone, when you visit it. Cookies allow a website to remember information about your visit, such as your language preference, the contents of your shopping cart, or that you have already logged in.

Similar technologies, such as pixels, tags, web beacons, software development kits and local storage, work in comparable ways and are covered by this Policy along with cookies.

A session cookie is deleted automatically once you close your browser. A persistent cookie remains on your device for a set period, or until you delete it, so that it can recognise you on a later visit.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

We use the following categories of cookies on the Platform:

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are required for the Platform to function and cannot be switched off. They include cookies that keep items in your shopping cart, remember that you are logged in, balance load across our servers, and protect the Platform against fraud and abuse. Because these cookies are essential to provide the service you have asked for, they are placed without asking for separate consent.

Functional Cookies

These cookies remember choices you make, such as your preferred language, currency or region, so that we can give you a more personal and convenient experience the next time you visit.

Performance and Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors use the Platform, such as which pages are viewed most often, how long a visit lasts, and whether an error occurs, so that we can measure and improve the performance of the Platform.

Advertising and Marketing Cookies

These cookies are used to show you products, offers and advertisements that are relevant to your interests, on the Platform and on other websites and apps, and to measure how well an advertising campaign is performing. They may also be used to limit how many times you see a given advertisement.

Functional, Performance and Analytics, and Advertising and Marketing cookies are not strictly necessary for the Platform to work, and are placed only with your consent, which you may give, refuse or withdraw at any time as described in Clause 7 below.

6. Consent and Legal Basis

Strictly Necessary Cookies are placed because they are needed to provide the Platform and the features you have asked for, and do not require separate consent.

Functional, Performance and Analytics, and Advertising and Marketing Cookies are placed only where you have given consent through the cookie banner or preference centre described in Clause 7. In line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, this consent is free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous, is given through a clear affirmative action such as ticking a box or selecting Accept, and is as easy to refuse or withdraw as it is to give.

You will not be denied access to the Platform or charged a different price simply because you choose to refuse cookies that are not strictly necessary, although some features that rely on those cookies, such as personalised recommendations, may not work as intended.

7. How to Manage or Withdraw Your Cookie Choices

You can manage your cookie choices for the Platform at any time through:

  • The cookie banner shown when you first visit the Platform;
  • The Cookie Settings or Manage Cookies link available in the footer of every page of the Platform, which opens the preference centre and lets you turn categories of cookies on or off and save your choices; and
  • Your browser settings, which allow you to block or delete cookies generally. Please refer to the help section of your browser, such as Chrome, Firefox, Safari or Edge, for instructions, as the steps differ between browsers and devices.

If you clear cookies from your browser, or use a new device or browser, you will need to set your cookie preferences again the next time you visit the Platform.

8. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" setting that sends a signal to websites requesting that they not track the browsing activity of that visitor. There is currently no uniform standard for how such signals should be read, and applicable Indian law does not require us to respond to them in a particular way. Orenkafine currently relies on the choices you make through the cookie banner and preference centre described in Clause 7, rather than on Do Not Track signals, to determine which cookies are placed on your device.

9. Children

The Platform is intended for use by individuals who are at least eighteen years of age, as described in the Orenkafine Terms & Conditions. We do not knowingly use cookies to track or profile children, or to serve targeted advertising to children, consistent with the protections for children under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
