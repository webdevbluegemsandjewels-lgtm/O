Questions, answered

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join the loyalty program?

In order to join the program, just click “Sign in” on the top right corner of our navigation menu and create an account. Once completed, you are welcome to participate in all actions we have prepared for you to earn points. You will be automatically opted into the OrenkaFine Loyalty Program after agreeing to our terms and conditions. If you already have an account, you will automatically be enrolled in the program and will begin earning points on your future purchases (just make sure you log into your account before completing your order).

It is free to join and customers are not obligated to participate and use points earned or accumulated. Once a customer deactivates their account, points will not be redeemable, transferable, or applicable to any future purchases after deactivation.

How long do I remain a member of the loyalty programme?

OrenkaFine membership is for lifetime. There is no downgrade once you become a member.

How can I earn loyalty points?

You can earn the points by completing actions listed in the rewards tab. Just click on the “Rewards” tab to see the list of activities available for you to participate in. You automatically earn 500 points just for signing up! You will earn 1 point for every ₹10 you spend on the website.

What is the conversion of these loyalty points?

10 loyalty points is worth 1 Rupee. So if you have 10,000 loyalty points, you can redeem it against your order for ₹1000.

What is the validity of the loyalty points?

Loyalty points expire 6 months from the day of credit to your account.

Can I combine loyalty points with other offers?

Loyalty points are like your money that you can combine with any offer, promotion or discount.

Can I combine loyalty points with other coupon codes?

Loyalty points can't be redeemed if there is an active coupon code applied to your cart.

How do I redeem my loyalty points?

10 points = ₹1. In order to redeem your OrenkaFine loyalty points, you will have to log into your account and during checkout select the amount you want to redeem to apply the points.

What is the maximum redeemable loyalty points valid for a single purchase?

You can redeem a maximum of 20,000 points.

How do I check my loyalty points balance?

Log into your account by clicking “Sign in” on the upper right-hand side of the screen. Enter your email address and password and check “My Account”. Your point balance will appear under the “My points” section.

How long will it take for the points to post to my account?

Your points will automatically post to your account after a purchase is completed from our end.

Can I transfer my loyalty points?

OrenkaFine loyalty points and membership are not transferrable. The facilities and benefits of the membership can be availed only by the member.

What happens if I return or cancel the order made using loyalty points?

When you return an item, you forfeit the associated points earned from the original purchase.

What if there is missing credit for a purchase?

If you do not see your points appear in your account after your original order was completed, please email us at contact@orenkafine.com.

What happens while returning a product under exchange policy?

Any points redeemed during a previous purchase will be deducted during Lifetime Exchange value and buy back calculations.
